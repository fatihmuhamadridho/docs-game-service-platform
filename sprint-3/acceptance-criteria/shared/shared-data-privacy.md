## Story

> As a business, I want to minimize and control access to protected data so that I can protect privacy and financial evidence.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-SHR-024

> **Given :** Customer, payment, or audit information is handled
> **When :** Information is stored or accessed
> **Then :**
>
