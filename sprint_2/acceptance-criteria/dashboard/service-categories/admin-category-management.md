# Admin Service Category Management

> **Document Type :** Acceptance Criteria
> **Status :** Draft

## Story

> As an authorized administrator, I want to manage service categories so that I can keep public navigation aligned with approved offerings.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-ADM-017

> **Given :** Administrator has catalog-management permission
> **When :** Administrator creates, views, updates, deactivates, or removes a category as allowed
> **Then :**
>
> 1. Public navigation includes eligible categories; inactive/removed/empty category is not a purchase choice

## Technical Flow

1. authorized administrator: Administrator creates, views, updates, deactivates, or removes a category as allowed.
2. System: Public navigation includes eligible categories; inactive/removed/empty category is not a purchase choice.
