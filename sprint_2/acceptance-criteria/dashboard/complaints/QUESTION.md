## Q-COM-001 — Complaint workflow and data handling

**Question:** Is complaint management in scope, and what intake channel, privacy/retention, SLA, status transitions, permissions, notifications, and persistence apply?

**Source / evidence gap:** Prototype contains seeded customer/contact/order details and message history. BRD has no complaint workflow and no backend contract is established.

**Affected ACs / routes:** [Admin Complaints Prototype](admin-complaints-prototype.md), `/admin/complaints`.

**Decision owner:** Product/operations roles are not identified in supplied sources.

**Impact:** Complaint data contains personal information; no real customer-data workflow should be inferred from seeded mock records.
