# Shared Data Policy Questions

## Q-SHR-001 — Retention and minimization rules

**Question:** What approved retention periods and deletion rules apply to customer, payment-evidence, and audit data, and which fields are necessary for each purpose?

**Source / evidence gap:** BR-025 requires privacy controls and the AC requires retention under approved policy, but supplied requirements do not specify periods, deletion triggers, or field-level minimization rules. BR-022 also requires audit records without defining their retention period.

**Affected ACs / routes:** [Customer, Payment, and Audit Data Privacy](../../../sprint_3/acceptance-criteria/shared/shared-data-privacy.md), [Administrative Audit Recording](shared-audit-recording.md), customer/payment/admin data handling.

**Decision owner:** Not identified in supplied sources.

**Impact:** Storage, redaction, deletion, and audit requirements cannot be finalized without an approved retention and minimization policy.
