# Cross-Sprint Route and Feature Coverage

> **Document type:** Acceptance-criteria coverage audit
> **Status:** Draft
> **Scope:** Cross-sprint route/page-to-AC traceability. Sprint 1 groups identity/access and games; Sprint 2 groups catalog/supporting features; Sprint 3 groups payment/order lifecycle and admin dashboard reads. Route evidence is not product approval.

This audit separates **solutioning coverage** (technical route/feature documentation) from **AC coverage** (BRD-backed outcomes and explicitly labeled candidate behavior). Sprint BRDs preserve original requirement IDs: [Sprint 1 access, identity, and games](../BRD.md), [Sprint 2 catalog and supporting features](../../sprint_2/BRD.md), [Sprint 3 payment and dashboard reads](../../sprint_3/BRD.md), and [separate BR-010 candidate](../../sprint_3/payment-evidence-BRD.md). Sprint placement is a planning proposal: it does not approve requirements, policies, estimates, or implementation. Route existence or technical coverage does not prove an approved requirement or completed implementation. An AC does not prove implementation or verification.

## Sprint grouping status

- [Sprint 1](../README.md): proposed access, user/role, and game foundation; seven stories; admin login verification, identity/RBAC, and catalog policy remain gated.
- [Sprint 2](../../sprint_2/README.md): proposed catalog/settings/audit and supporting public group; 22 stories including explicitly gated/candidate stories.
- [Sprint 3](../../sprint_3/README.md): proposed payment/order lifecycle and dashboard/read consumers; 12 stories, with payment policy, BR-010, persisted data, privacy, and metrics gated.
- The sprint-specific BRDs retain the original BR wording; the [root README](../../README.md#sprint-grouping-and-status) records proposed sequencing.

## Interpretation and limits

- “Solutioning covered” identifies technical documentation only; it is not product approval.
- “AC covered” means a BRD-backed intended outcome has a story document; it does not establish code completion, deployment, or test verification.
- BR-010 payment evidence is in the candidate Sprint 3 BRD while Go upload/finalize routes are gated. Keep requirement traceable and resolve release scope with owners.
- Go inventory reports admin-account creation returns 503 because identity invitation is unavailable. The AC records intended behavior and does not claim this route is complete.
