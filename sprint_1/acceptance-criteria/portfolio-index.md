# Cross-Sprint Acceptance Criteria Index

Actor-based story documents follow the project’s TEMPLATE-AC structure and live in feature-specific folders under their proposed sprint. BRD-backed stories retain their requirement IDs. Prototype-derived stories record visible behavior and open scope decisions; they do not prove implementation, approval, or production readiness. The three-sprint order moves identity/access and games early, catalog/supporting work to the middle, and payment lifecycle plus admin read/dashboard consumers last. Shared authorization appears once in Sprint 1 as an early prerequisite; retry safety and privacy remain late cross-sprint controls.
