# Admin Role and Permission Management

> **Document Type :** Acceptance Criteria
> **Status :** Draft

## Story

> As an authorized administrator, I want to manage roles and permissions so that I can maintain least-privilege access.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-ADM-015

> **Given :** Administrator has role-management permission
> **When :** Administrator views or changes roles/permissions
> **Then :**
>
> 1. Allowed changes follow approved least privilege; protected roles and unauthorized delegation cannot be changed

## Technical Flow

1. authorized administrator: Administrator views or changes roles/permissions.
2. System: Allowed changes follow approved least privilege; protected roles and unauthorized delegation cannot be changed.
