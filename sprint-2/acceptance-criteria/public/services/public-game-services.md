# Public Services for a Game

> **Document Type :** Acceptance Criteria
> **Status :** Draft

## Story

> As a Customer, I want to review active services for a selected game and their pricing type so that I can choose an eligible service without relying on an unapproved price.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-PUB-003

> **Given :** A game is available
> **When :** Customer views its services or attempts to proceed with a quote-required service
> **Then :**
>
> 1. Active services show fixed-price or quote-required status; no unapproved price is payable

## Technical Flow

1. Customer: Customer views its services or attempts to proceed with a quote-required service.
2. System: Active services show fixed-price or quote-required status; no unapproved price is payable.
