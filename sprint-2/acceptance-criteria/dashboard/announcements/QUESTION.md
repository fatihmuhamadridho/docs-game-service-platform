# Announcements Questions

## Q-ANN-001 — Announcement capability and publication rules

**Question:** Is announcement management in scope, and what audience, scheduling/publication, persistence, and transition rules apply?

**Source / evidence gap:** AdminAnnouncements exposes types, audiences, and draft/scheduled/published states over seeded local mock rows. BRD has no announcement requirement or backend route.

**Affected ACs / routes:** [Admin Announcements Prototype](admin-announcements-prototype.md), `/admin/announcements`.

**Impact:** Prototype controls must not be treated as persisted or publishable capability until scope and rules are approved.
